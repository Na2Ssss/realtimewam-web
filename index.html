THE IDEA

Compute where it matters.
Overlap what can run together.

World action models predict actions—and sometimes future video—from the current observation. Their inference cost can leave a robot waiting while its environment continues to move.

RealtimeWAM coordinates three decisions: when each branch runs, which observation tokens need fresh features, and how many full Transformer evaluations a prediction receives.

We evaluate the framework on FastWAM and OpenWAM across RoboTwin, LIBERO, LIBERO-Plus, and five real-world tasks.

IN ACTION

METHOD

One coordinated inference pipeline.

The execution schedule and the amount of fresh computation are designed together.

01

Overlap execution

Release observation keys and values layer by layer, allowing prediction to overlap the remaining observation computation.

02

Refresh selected tokens

Use RGB and feature changes to select observation updates. Fill available execution capacity with informative tokens.

03

Adapt refinement

Decode a coarse action after two full evaluations. Predicted displacement determines whether to add two more.
